# Origami Health — hands-on durations

Each hands-on session lasts 80 minutes. Half-Day 1 includes a 20-minute setup check (allow 15–20 minutes); the remaining 60 minutes cover the labs. Separate slide decks, breaks and Q&A are outside this guide. Keep the complete workshop day within three hours; start times are flexible and are not displayed.

## Half-Day 1: Build the trusted data foundation — 80 minutes total

Audience: Data Management, data engineers, architects and governance leads

| Duration | Activity | Format |
|---:|---|---|
| 20 min | Setup and access check | Setup |
| 25 min | Ingest and refine | Hands-on · Lab 1 |
| 25 min | Gold and AI Lakehouse | Hands-on · Lab 2 |
| 10 min | Governance, workflow and validation | Hands-on checkpoint |

Outcome: Validated Bronze/Silver data and governed Claims and Benefits views in AI Lakehouse, ready for the second session.

## Half-Day 2: From claims signals to benefits assistance — 80 minutes total

Audience: Data scientists, business and Member Operations representatives, with technical facilitators

| Duration | Activity | Format |
|---:|---|---|
| 15 min | Claims anomaly scoring | Hands-on · Lab 3 |
| 15 min | Benefits Knowledge Base | Hands-on · Lab 4 |
| 40 min | Member Benefits Inquiry Copilot | Hands-on · Lab 5 |
| 10 min | Human-in-the-loop action | Hands-on challenge |

Outcome: An investigator review queue, a Benefits Knowledge Base, a Member Benefits Inquiry Copilot and a human-reviewed service brief.
